# 🥠 Random Numbers Fortune Cookie

Crack a virtual fortune cookie and get random lucky numbers, your lucky
percentage, zodiac sign, lucky color, lucky time and fengshui direction —
all in one tap!

**Play it here:** https://barrystlow.github.io/Fortune_numberpicker/

## ✨ Features

- 🥠 Tap anywhere to crack the cookie
- 🎲 Choose number length from **1 to 100 digits**
- 🔢 Generate **1 to 100 numbers** at once
- 🍀 Lucky Percentage meter (90%+ = 🔥 HOT results!)
- ⚡ 15% chance of a MEGA HUAT LUCKY SURPRISE jackpot
- 🧧 Zodiac, lucky color, lucky time & fengshui direction with every crack
- 📜 History of your last 20 cracks with lucky bars
- 💰 Huat-style fortunes ("HUAT AH!!! ONG for you ah!")
- 📱 Fully mobile-friendly

## 🎮 How to Use

1. Open the site (link above)
2. Pick your number length and how many numbers you want
3. Tap anywhere on the page to crack the cookie
4. View your numbers, lucky stats and fortune!
